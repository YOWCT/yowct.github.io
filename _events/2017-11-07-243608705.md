Come celebrate Ottawa Civic Tech's 1-year anniversary!
You are invited to Ottawa Civic Tech's weekly "hacknights", held every Tuesday evening from 6:00-8:30pm. Free and open to all, hacknights bring together people from all walks of life to connect, learn, share, and collaborate.
You do not need to be a "techie" to attend - everyone is welcome to participate and contribute to building civic good through technology, data, design, policy, advocacy & citizen engagement. Join us!
The first Tuesday of the month always features a guest speaker. Other weeks may also feature a guest speaker, but is primarily meant to provide dedicated time and space for the community to make progress on their projects.
Agenda:
6:00-6:30pm -- Doors Open; Networking
6:30-7:00pm -- Welcome; Presentation from guest speaker and Q&A
7:00-7:20pm -- Introductions
7:20-7:30pm -- Project updates + new pitches (bonus: representatives from the Ministry of Advanced Education and Skills Development will be present to answer questions about the Student Pathways Challenge (https://studentpathwayschallenge.ca/)!)
7:30-8:20pm -- Hacknight (breakout group work sessions)
8:20-8:30pm -- Wrap-up
Guest speakers: Mike Colledge and Jen Birch, President and Vice President of Public Affairs at Ipsos Canada (https://www.ipsos.com/en-ca/CanadaNext-2017)
With over 20 years in the business, Mike has worked with leading governments and businesses to use evidence to drive change. Jennifer Birch, Vice President at Ipsos Public Affairs Canada is a seasoned research professional with over a decade of experience in using data to help organizations succeed. Together, they will join us to discuss the results of a recent Ipsos study called CanadaNext (https://www.ipsos.com/en-ca/CanadaNext-2017).
Ipsos CanadaNext looks at the wave of change about to happen across Canada. Driven by demographic shifts and technology advancement, our economy, our communities, our governments, our businesses, and our lives are about to change drastically. Find out what Canadians expect to happen and how they feel about it.
