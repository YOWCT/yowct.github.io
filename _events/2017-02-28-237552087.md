This week, we'll do an 'off-cycle' presentation; our guest speaker is Walter Knitl, Principal at Praxiem. Walter will speak to us on "Internet of Things - Impact and Opportunity as Civic Tech".
Abstract: The Internet of Things (IoT) age is upon us with disruptive impact on business, work life, economy and society. But what is it exactly, what impact can it have on society, and what opportunities does it present for social good?
Bio: Walter is the principal at Praxiem, with main interest in the Internet of Things (IoT) - including immediate business and product application as well as societal impacts. He helped organize the IoT613 Conference and is the founder and administrator of IoT eXchange Ottawa.
@praxiem (http://twitter.com/praxiem) / http://praxiem.com / @iotottawa (http://twitter.com/iotxottawa)
About Ottawa Civic Tech: we meet weekly on Tuesdays: the first night of the month kicks off with a presentation from a guest speaker and project updates/outlook for the month; the rest of the month will be focused on "hacking" (working on our projects to build civic good).
