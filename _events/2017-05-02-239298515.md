==============
Jason White (https://twitter.com/Actinolite) joined the data team at Shopify in 2012, and has led data analytics teams for support, business development, and sales functions at Shopify. He is now a data engineering lead helping to build the next generation of Shopify's data platform.
We'll go through an example of a Python scraper for the CMHC web site, which has housing information for most municipalities across Canada.
