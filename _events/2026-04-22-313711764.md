Join us for Ottawa Civic Tech’s next hacknight. A welcoming space for anyone interested in using tech to do civic good.
Our members bring all kinds of skills, like design, policy, community engagement, software development, data, and more to explore ideas, solve tough problems, and make our communities better, together.

**Agenda (EST):**
6:00 p.m. — Networking
6:30 p.m. — Welcome + introductions
7:00 p.m. — Breakouts and project discussions

**More about Ottawa Civic Tech**
Using tech to do civic good. Ottawa Civic Tech (#YOWCT) connects people across sectors and silos to solve tough problems and make our communities better together.
Everyone is welcome, whether you want to learn, meet new people, share your expertise, or explore how you might contribute.
