To round out the year, Ottawa Civic Tech is hosting an evening of demos from community members. Sessions may include Random Hacks of Kindness project updates, mini talks or project updates from Ottawa Civic Tech-ers, or other demos of other projects in the civic good space.
Ottawa Civic Tech "hacknights" are held Tuesday evenings once or twice a month from 6:00-8:00pm. Free and open to all, hacknights bring together people from all walks of life to connect, learn, share, and collaborate.
You do not need to be a "techie" to attend - everyone is welcome to participate and contribute to building civic good through technology, data, design, policy, advocacy & citizen engagement. Join us!
