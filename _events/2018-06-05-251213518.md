You are invited to Ottawa Civic Tech's weekly "hacknights", held every Tuesday evening from 6:00-8:30pm. Free and open to all, hacknights bring together people from all walks of life to connect, learn, share, and collaborate.
You do not need to be a "techie" to attend - everyone is welcome to participate and contribute to building civic good through technology, data, design, policy, advocacy & citizen engagement. Join us!
Typically, the first Tuesday of the month features a guest speaker. Other weeks may also feature a guest speaker, but is primarily meant to provide dedicated time and space for the community to make progress on their projects.
==============
About Our Guest Speaker
Shelley Robinson is Executive Director of National Capital FreeNet, a not-for-profit Internet Service Provider that was established in 1992. NCF is a social enterprise that believes everyone in the region should have access to the internet, meaning they can afford a high quality connection, understand how to use it, and feel safe online. What does that look like in practice?
FreeNet is one of the founders of Digital Access Day, a free day-long conference on Tuesday June 19th at City Hall to discuss the ways the digital divide too often mirrors and exacerbates existing inequities. Shelley will also talk about the conference and ways people can contribute to that work. Register at: www.digitalaccessday.ca
Shelley's talk will be livestreamed - tune in at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r5PqkX6u8xw
