During the summer, Ottawa Civic Tech hosts informal working sessions for those who wish to continue working on their civic tech projects, looking to join up with those working on projects, and/or looking to connect to the community. Everyone is welcome!
We'll do these summer sessions at coffee shops, restaurants, and patios to allow for social space as well as food and beverages. Please try to RSVP if you can so that we can manage numbers and potential venue reservations/estimates for seating. You’re still very welcome if you didn't have a chance to RSVP. Hope to see you there!
