Ottawa Civic Tech (YOWCT) is a community of citizens from diverse sectors and experiences who are interested in better understanding our complex social and civic challenges and building solutions at the intersection of technology, design, data, policy, entrepreneurship, advocacy, and citizen engagement. First launched in 2016, Ottawa Civic Tech took a break for obvious reasons in early 2020 - now it's time to revive it!
This will be a networking event for anyone interested in Ottawa Civic Tech, in reconnecting with each other, and in helping determine a future direction.
