Join us for Ottawa Civic Tech’s last meetup before our summer break! This won’t be a regular hacknight, instead, we’ll come together to celebrate the progress, momentum, and impact our community has built over the past season.
The evening will feature project demos from community members, plenty of time to connect and socialize, and a chance to reflect on what we’ve accomplished together.

**Agenda (EST)**

* 6:00 p.m. — Networking

* 6:30 p.m. — Welcome + introductions

* 7:00 p.m. — [Project demos](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Utp73r8vmRxp51NTOU7H2iVrf1Tpipwn1kPf2fpQU7Q/edit?usp=sharing)

* 8:00 p.m. — Social time

**More about Ottawa Civic Tech**
Using tech to do civic good. Ottawa Civic Tech (#YOWCT) connects people across sectors and silos to solve tough problems and make our communities better together.
Everyone is welcome, whether you want to learn, meet new people, share your expertise, or explore how you might contribute.
