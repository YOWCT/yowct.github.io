We meet weekly on Tuesdays: the first night of the month kicks off with a presentation from a guest speaker and project updates/outlook for the month; the rest of the month will be focused on "hacking" (working on our projects to build civic good). Agenda for Tuesday, April 4th:
6:00 - 6:30pm Networking (feel free to bring your dinner!)
6:30 - 7:00pm Welcome + Intros
7:00 - 7:15pm Presentation from guest speaker
7:15 - 7:30pm Q&A
7:30 - 7:50pm Project updates & new pitches
7:50 - 8:20pm Hackportion with breakout conversations & group work
8:20pm - 8:30pm Wrap Up

This month's guest speaker is John Richardson, CEO of Vancouver-based Ethelo, who has graciously accepted to speak to YOWCT while in Ottawa.
More about Ethelo here: http://ethelo.com/
Ethelo’s powerful algorithmic technology harnesses the powers of optimized consensus and scenario analysis within a comprehensive engagement framework.
