We meet weekly on Tuesdays: the first night of the month kicks off with a presentation from a guest speaker and project updates/outlook for the month; the rest of the month will be focused on "hacking" (working on our projects to build civic good).
Agenda for Tuesday, June 6th:
6:00pm Networking (feel free to bring your dinner!)
6:30 - 7:00pm Welcome + Intros
7:00 - 7:20pm Presentation from guest speaker
7:20 - 7:30pm Q&A
7:30 - 7:50pm Project updates & new pitches
7:50 - 8:20pm Hackportion with breakout conversations & group work
8:20 - 8:30pm Wrap Up

==============
Guest speaker: Tracey Lauriault
Tracey Lauriault will give a talk about understanding spacial media, data and the city, and the role of civic tech in a smart city (and particularly precision agriculture). Tracey (@TraceyLauriault) is Assistant Professor of Critical Media Studies and Big Data in the Communication Studies School of Journalism and Communication at Carleton University.
