**Let’s celebrate a great year of reconnecting, collaborating, and exploring how technology can support civic good.**
For this session, we're going to focus on creating social time and space instead of a guest speaker, as the last Ottawa Civic Tech meetup before taking a summer break.
Over the past 13 months, we’ve rebuilt momentum, welcomed fresh faces, and uncovered opportunities to make a positive impact—and we’re just getting started. Whether you’ve been with Ottawa Civic Tech since the early days or are just discovering the group, this is a chance to come together, share what we’ve learned, and take a quick look at what’s coming next.

**Agenda**
6:30 PM – Open networking
7:00 PM – Welcome and introductions
7:15 PM – A quick look back at 13 months of Ottawa Civic Tech and a quick look ahead
7:30 PM – Social

**More about Ottawa Civic Tech**
Using tech to do civic good. Ottawa Civic Tech (#YOWCT) connects people across sectors and silos to solve tough problems and make our communities better together.
As a civic tech group we provide a space for motivated individuals to connect and create projects for civic good. Community members bring their interests, insights, or skillsets like design, policy, community engagement, software development, and more.
All are welcome, including if you just want to learn and meet people, or if you’re not yet sure how you can contribute.
