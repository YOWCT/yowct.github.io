Join us for a special community meetup, co-organized with the Rideau-Rockcliffe Community Resource Centre, where the team behind *Community in a Box* will share their bold vision for a new kind of digital platform — one that is owned, governed, and shaped by the communities it serves.
You’ll get to see a **live demo of their progress**, hear about their vision, share your ideas, and find out how you can get involved in building a healthier digital future for our neighbourhood, from the ground up.
For more information about the project, visit: *[Community in a Box](https://luclalande.medium.com/community-in-a-box-incubation-and-barn-raising-f5a8f57e4d02)*

**More about Ottawa Civic Tech**
Using tech to do civic good. Ottawa Civic Tech (#YOWCT) connects people across sectors and silos to solve tough problems and make our communities better together.
As a civic tech group we provide a space for motivated individuals to connect and create projects for civic good. Community members bring their interests, insights, or skillsets like design, policy, community engagement, software development, and more.
All are welcome, including if you just want to learn and meet people, or if you’re not yet sure how you can contribute.
