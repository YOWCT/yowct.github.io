We meet weekly on Tuesdays: the first night of the month kicks off with a presentation from a guest speaker and project updates/outlook for the month; the rest of the month will be focused on "hacking" (working on our projects to build civic good).
Agenda for Tuesday, June 27th:
6:30pm Networking (feel free to bring your dinner!)
6:30 - 7:00pm Welcome + Intros
7:00 - 7:20pm Presentation from Code for Canada
7:20 - 7:30pm Q&A
7:30 - 7:50pm Project updates & new pitches
7:50 - 8:20pm Hackportion with breakout conversations & group work
8:20 - 8:30pm Wrap Up
Join our Slack group: https://yowct-invite-bot.herokuapp.com/
====
Special guest: Code for Canada (http://codefor.ca/)
Code for Canada is a national nonprofit that's connecting government innovators with members of the (civic) tech and design community, so they can build better digital public services together. Lia Milito (https://twitter.com/liabadia) and Luke Simcoe (https://twitter.com/Code4Luke) from Code for Canada will present the latest news from the organization – including an exciting new opportunity for Ottawa technologists to participate in their fellowship program – and share ways to get involved.
====
As this is our last formal Meetup before the start of the summer season, we'll plan on going for drinks afterwards in the market nearby. Feel free to invite friends and colleagues to join!
During July and August, we'll continue meeting informally (in coffee shops etc.) on Tuesdays as people are interested to work on projects. Stay tuned to the YOWCT Slack channel (https://yowcivictech.slack.com/) for updates on where we'll be. Our regularly-scheduled Meetups will be back at the start of September.
