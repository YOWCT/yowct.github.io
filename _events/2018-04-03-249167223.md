You are invited to Ottawa Civic Tech's weekly "hacknights", held every Tuesday evening from 6:00-8:30pm. Free and open to all, hacknights bring together people from all walks of life to connect, learn, share, and collaborate.
You do not need to be a "techie" to attend - everyone is welcome to participate and contribute to building civic good through technology, data, design, policy, advocacy & citizen engagement. Join us!
The first Tuesday of the month always features a guest speaker. Other weeks may also feature a guest speaker, but is primarily meant to provide dedicated time and space for the community to make progress on their projects.
==============
Our guest speaker this week: Christine Earnshaw
Christine has twenty years experience working in community and international development with a focus on public engagement. Driven by an interest to make Ottawa more liveable, equitable and sustainable, she seeks collaborations with others to catalyze ideas into action. In 2014, Christine co-founded Tree Fest Ottawa, an Ottawa-based nonprofit that celebrates trees and engages the public and policymakers on the importance of the urban forest. Since 2015, she has been on the Board of Directors of the Parkdale Food Centre, a progressive food centre in downtown Ottawa. This is her second year working with Synapcity as Project Lead for 100in1Day.
100in1Day is a global festival featuring hundreds of citizen-led interventions in public spaces all on one day. In 2018, 100in1Day is taking place on June 2nd in over 40 cities around the world. Powered by Synapcity, this is Ottawa's second year taking part. During this presentation/workshop, we will look at examples of interventions featuring technology and design that were implemented last year in Ottawa and in other cities. Participants will then have an opportunity to generate ideas and plan ways to take part in 100in1Day as a means to engage citizens on the use of technology and design to address complex social and civic challenges.
