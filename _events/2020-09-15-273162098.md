Hi Civic Tech Ottawa members,
Would you like to get more involved and help in co-organizing Civic Tech Ottawa for the next little while? If so, please join us for a Zoom meeting for organizers (and those interested in becoming organizers) on Tuesday September 15th 6-7pm. You will see the Zoom link in this Meetup once you RSVP.
We have a few key organizers who are continuing to support but we are looking to bring more organizers on and to get ideas from those interested in co-organizing with us. Everyone is welcome with the only requirement being that you are looking to contribute to an inclusive civic tech community. There are opportunities to take on small and larger roles. We anticipate most if not all gatherings and supporting work will be online for the coming months. We’ll start with a roundtable discussion on people’s ideas for the coming fall and spring. We hope to see you there! If you are interested in helping with organizing but are unable to make it to this meeting, please shoot us a message or join the #organizing channel on the YOW Civic Tech Slack (https://join.slack.com/t/yowcivictech/shared_invite/zt-h3r1au3r-J9Gf~qRQnuO3hT~9fKKY5w) and we'll try to loop you in.
