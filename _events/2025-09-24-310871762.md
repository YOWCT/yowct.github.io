We’re back! Join us as we kick off a new season of Ottawa Civic Tech—bringing together motivated individuals to connect, collaborate, and build projects for civic good.
This time, there’s no guest speaker. Instead, we’re making space for informal conversations, small group discussions, project updates, and idea-sharing.
Bring your interests, insights, and skills—whether in design, policy, software development, community engagement, activism, or beyond—and join a welcoming community of people working to make a positive impact.

**Agenda**
6:30 PM – Doors open & casual networking
7:00 PM – Welcome + quick community update
7:15 PM – Open social time

**More about Ottawa Civic Tech**
Using tech to do civic good. Ottawa Civic Tech (#YOWCT) connects people across sectors and silos to solve tough problems and make our communities better together.
As a civic tech group we provide a space for motivated individuals to connect and create projects for civic good. Community members bring their interests, insights, or skillsets like design, policy, community engagement, software development, and more.
All are welcome, including if you just want to learn and meet people, or if you’re not yet sure how you can contribute.
