Join us for an evening of ideas, innovation, and inspiration at the intersection of civic tech and public service.
Sam Burton will kick off the evening by sharing new research from the Government of Canada’s Canadian Digital Service on how civic tech can help improve government service delivery. Then, we’ll hear from Claudie Larouche, a passionate builder of community tools, who will share how she uses technology to make everyday life easier for parents and families in Ottawa. We’ll round it out with Parth Shah’s presentation of Canadian Shield, CIRA’s free, public DNS resolver for family protection at home.

**Agenda (EST):**

* 6:00 p.m. - Networking

* 6:30 p.m. - Welcome + introductions

* 7:00 p.m. - Presentations - Sam Burton, Claudie Larouche, Parth Shah

* 7:45 p.m. - Breakout and project discussions

**More about Ottawa Civic Tech**
Using tech to do civic good. Ottawa Civic Tech (#YOWCT) connects people across sectors and silos to solve tough problems and make our communities better together.
As a civic tech group we provide a space for motivated individuals to connect and create projects for civic good. Community members bring their interests, insights, or skillsets like design, policy, community engagement, software development, and more.
All are welcome, including if you just want to learn and meet people, or if you’re not yet sure how you can contribute.
