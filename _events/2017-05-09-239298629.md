We meet weekly on Tuesdays: the first night of the month kicks off with a presentation from a guest speaker and project updates/outlook for the month; the rest of the month will be focused on "hacking" (working on our projects to build civic good).
Agenda for Tuesday, May 9th:
6:30pm Networking (feel free to bring your dinner!)
6:30 - 7:00pm Welcome + Intros
7:00 - 7:20pm Presentation from guest speaker
7:20 - 7:30pm Q&A
7:30 - 7:50pm Project updates & new pitches
7:50 - 8:20pm Hackportion with breakout conversations & group work
8:20 - 8:30pm Wrap Up

==============
Guest speaker: Jason White
Jason White (https://twitter.com/Actinolite) joined the data team at Shopify in 2012, and has led data analytics teams for support, business development, and sales functions at Shopify. He is now a data engineering lead helping to build the next generation of Shopify's data platform.
We'll go through an example of a Python scraper for the CMHC web site, which has housing information for most municipalities across Canada.
