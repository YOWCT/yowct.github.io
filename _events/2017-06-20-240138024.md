Guest speakers: Laura Wesley and Sean Kibbee
Meeting citizen expectations to participate in decision-making means working together across organizational boundaries to advance shared outcomes. To help deliver on the Government of Canada's commitment to Open Dialogue / Open Policy Making, we are collaborating with civic tech and public engagement practitioners to co-create an eco-system of tools for citizens and government to engage online.
We will use this time to bring the prototype and the “behind the scenes” workings to civic tech, as we continue building out this proof of concept.
Laura Wesley (https://twitter.com/ResultsJunkie) is the Executive Director of the Consultations and Public Engagement division of the Privy Council Office. Laura has a track record of disrupting the status quo to bring about results. Her knowledge of, and interest in, systems change, service design, and human motivation form a lens through which to consider new ways of working together. After more than a decade working in the federal public service, she's come to believe that working across boundaries – sectors, disciplines and organizations – results in better outcomes for everyone.
Sean Kibbee (https://twitter.com/SDKibb) is a Free Agent with Natural Resources Canada working as a Senior Systems Analyst within TBS. He has over 15 years experience in government IT. Sean specializes in mobile app technologies and leveraging open source for creative solutions within the Federal Government.
