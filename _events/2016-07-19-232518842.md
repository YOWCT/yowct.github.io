YOWCT Pilot Session #1
Location: PCO Central Innovation Hub, 90 Sparks (Sparks/Metcalfe), 8th floor, Unit #820.
Please note that the floors become inaccessible to the public after 6pm, so we’ll have one of us waiting for you downstairs when you get into the building to activate the elevators.
Time: come anytime after 6:30pm - we’ll start the formal part of the evening at 7:00pm; we will end by 9:15pm at the latest.
Should you eat beforehand? Yes. We can quickly discuss how we want to tackle food when we meet (e.g. uber eats, brown bag), but for this initial session, we won’t be providing food. However, if you want to bring some snacks to share, please do!
Proposed agenda (feel free to modify/edit/comment)
Welcome Dan 10 mins
Initial vision & possibilities
Introductions All 20 minutes
Q to answer: who are you? why you are here?
Guest speaker Ryan Androsoff on digital government 15 minutes
5-10 mins Q/A after the talk
Forming conversation Dan (moderator) 50 minutes
Civic Tech ‘101’ (adapted from C.T. TO)
Roundtable discussion:
Q to answer: name one thing you want this group to be and do (i.e. what are you excited for?)
Q to answer: name one thing don't you want this group to be and do (i.e. what should we avoid?)
Note: we’ll need a note taker for this portion
Do part All 30 minutes
Roundtable discussion:
Q to answer: what are you working on right now? What do you need to make it happen? (access to resources, skills, people)
