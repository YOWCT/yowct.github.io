Guest speaker: Wendy Luciani from the Canadian Digital Service!
Wendy Luciani is the partnerships lead for the Canadian Digital Service (https://digital.canada.ca/). She leads CDS’ project selection process, working with departments to identify and select digital service projects to work on together.
Prior to joining CDS, Wendy led Traveller Transformation at the Canada Border Services Agency, and launched the eDeclaration mobile app, which replaced the paper arrival forms with an offline-capable smartphone solution.
In her free time, if she isn't on a mountain or a lake, she's busy raising two awesome little humans.
