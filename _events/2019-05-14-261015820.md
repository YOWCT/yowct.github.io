You are invited to Ottawa Civic Tech's weekly "hacknights", held every Tuesday evening from 6:00-8:00pm. Free and open to all, hacknights bring together people from all walks of life to connect, learn, share, and collaborate.
You do not need to be a "techie" to attend - everyone is welcome to participate and contribute to building civic good through technology, data, design, policy, advocacy & citizen engagement. Join us!
==============
Agenda:
6:00-6:30pm -- Doors Open; Networking
6:30-6:45pm -- Welcome and Introductions
6:45-7:30pm -- Presentation
7:30-8:00pm -- Pitch Updates
==============
Our speaker this evening will be Matt Jukes, who has had a long career as a public servant including running a major digital transformation programme at the UK's Office for National Statistics, leading product management at civic tech organization mySociety and is now at Notbinary helping institutions apply the culture, practices, processes & technologies of the Internet-era to respond to their users raised expectations.
He will outline the importance of working in the open at an individual, team and organizational level in the public service (and beyond) to build trust with users and colleagues while increasing the understanding and acceptance of modern ways of working.
Illustrated by examples from the Office for National Statistics, GDS, UK Parliament, 18F in the US, DTA in Australia, Friends of the Earth, Mozilla, Gitlab, Netflix and more.
