We meet weekly on Tuesdays: the first night of the month kicks off with a presentation from a guest speaker and project updates/outlook for the month; the rest of the month will be focused on "hacking" (working on our projects to build civic good).
Agenda for Tuesday, May 23rd:
6:00-6:30pm -- Networking
6:30-7:00pm -- Welcome + Intros
7:00-7:15pm -- Guest presentation from Ryan Tumilty
7:15-7:25pm -- Q&A
7:25-8:25pm -- Hacknight
8:25-8:30pm -- Wrap-up

====
Ryan Tumilty is a senior reporter with Metro covering politics at all levels of government. He enjoys books, beer and naps.
He will be speaking about his data journalism and civic tech-related project called "Not in Service" ( http://www.metronews.ca/news/ottawa/2017/05/14/how-metro-created-a-database-of-cancelled-bus-trips-.html ), which takes a close look at the bus cancellations that seem to ripple across the transit system by collecting data that's tweeted out by OC Transpo.
