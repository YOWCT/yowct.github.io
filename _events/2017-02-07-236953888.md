We meet weekly on Tuesdays: the first night of the month kicks off with a presentation from a guest speaker and project updates/outlook for the month; the rest of the month will be focused on "hacking" (working on our projects to build civic good).
Agenda for Tuesday, January 17:
6:00pm - 6:30pm Network, eat (food provided by you)
6:30pm - 7:00pm Welcome + Intros
7:00pm - 7:30pm Presentation from Kevin O'Donnell about Ottwatch
7:30-8:20pm Hackportion + Office Hours
8:20pm-8:30pm Wrap Up
Ottawa Watch: The presentation will provide a short history of how OttWatch (developed from its first edition to its current form, the data gathering process, integration of data from multiple 'silos', and how the general public has contributed to improving the data from time to time.
Bio: Kevin O'Donnell is an information technology consultant with an interest in city issues. He runs OttWatch (http://ottwatch.ca), a website and Twitter account that tries to make it easier for people to connect with Ottawa's decision making process.
