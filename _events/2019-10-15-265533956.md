You are invited to Ottawa Civic Tech's weekly "hacknights", held Tuesday evenings from 6:00-8:00pm. Free and open to all, hacknights bring together people from all walks of life to connect, learn, share, and collaborate.
You do not need to be a "techie" to attend - everyone is welcome to participate and contribute to building civic good through technology, data, design, policy, advocacy & citizen engagement. Join us!
==============
Special guest: City of Ottawa Open Data and GIS teams
This informal session will have 3 components:
1) Back on April 2, city staff presented their "Digital Services Strategy Update". We will get an update from city staff on what they heard since then for the production rollout of https://open.ottawa.ca
2) Geospatial data makes up a large part of what is available as open data from the city. The city manages GIS information in a platform called geoOttawa, which also available to the public at http://maps.ottawa.ca/geoottawa/. The city's GIS team will share the plan for the upgrade for geoOttawa.
3) After the presentations you will be able to try out the new GeoOttawa and provide feedback directly to city staff.
We're really looking forward to having Tim, Matt and others from the city at this CivicTech meeting to share their experiences.
