You are invited to Ottawa Civic Tech's weekly "hacknights", held every Tuesday evening from 6:00-8:00pm. Free and open to all, hacknights bring together people from all walks of life to connect, learn, share, and collaborate.
You do not need to be a "techie" to attend - everyone is welcome to participate and contribute to building civic good through technology, data, design, policy, advocacy & citizen engagement. Join us!
Typically, the first Tuesday of the month features a guest speaker. Other weeks may also feature a guest speaker, but is primarily meant to provide dedicated time and space for the community to make progress on their projects.
==============
Agenda:
6:00pm - 6:30pm -- Doors Open; Networking
6:30pm - 6:45pm -- Welcome and Introductions
7:00pm - 7:30pm -- Presentation from Code for Canada
7:30pm - 7:45pm -- Project updates + Pitches
7:45pm - 8:30pm -- Hacknight (breakout group work sessions)
==============
Speakers: Code for Canada Fellows - Daniel Tse, Siobhan Ozege, Caley Brock and Fatima Khalid
Code for Canada is a national nonprofit that's connecting government innovators with members of the civic tech and design community, so they can build better digital public services together.
(http://codefor.ca/)
Fellows from Code for Canada will present the latest news from the organization – including an exciting opportunity for Ottawa technologists to participate in the Code for Canada Fellowship Program – and share ways to get involved.
