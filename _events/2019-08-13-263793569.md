As summer comes to a close, Ottawa Civic Tech would like to organize our plan for Fall 2019/Winter 2020.
We invite anyone who wants to join and take on a leadership role in Ottawa Civic Tech to participate, volunteer and commit a bit of their time to help us where our next events will be hosted. We'd like to crowdsource suggestions for interesting civic leaders that would make great speakers at our events as well the next unique civic project suggestions that we should focus on.
We welcome all event organizers, developers, analysts, civicl servants, social media and comms professionals, community advocates and civic tech enthusiasts!
If you can't physically be there, no worries! Feel free to contribute to the draft schedule/calendar sometime this week or whenever you can anytime before our Meetup:
