[UPDATE 1] Along with our usual weekly meetup, we are also having an organizers meeting to plan out our Fall activities for the YOWCT community. Anyone who is interested in getting more involved as a volunteer organizer is welcome to join us! Making YOWCT happen is a team effort - we are always looking for support in everything from booking speakers and venues, digital communications, partnerships and community outreach, etc.
[UPDATE 2] Please be advised that there is another event taking place at Impact Hub at the same time, so please be mindful of noise and resist the strong temptation to sample any refreshments from/for the other group! Everyone is welcome to bring their own food/drink - we have a microwave and Bridgehead coffee in our communal kitchen.
====
During the summer, Ottawa Civic Tech will be hosting informal working sessions for those who wish to continue working on their civic tech projects, looking to join up with those working on projects, and/or looking to connect to the community. Everyone is welcome!
We'll be trying to do these summer sessions at coffee shops, restaurants, and patios to also allow for social space as well as food and beverages. Please try to RSVP if you can so that we can manage numbers and potential venue reservations/estimates for seating. You are still very welcome if you didn't have a chance to RSVP. Hope to see you there!
