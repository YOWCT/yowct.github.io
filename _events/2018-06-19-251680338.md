This week's meeting will be a special collision with our friends running Digital Access Day.
Digital Access Day, a day-long conference to dive into these issues, including connectivity in Indigenous communities, digital literacy, research on the digital divide, and the challenges of making internet and connected devices affordable for everyone.
From 5:30-7:30pm Digital Access Day attendees are continuing the conversation at Shopify's offices (150 Elgin St), across the street from City Hall.
This week's Ottawa Civic Tech will be at the Digital Access Day reception.
