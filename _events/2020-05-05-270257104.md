Event description:
Our world has changed dramatically in the past few weeks as a result of the unprecedented COVID-19 pandemic. Governments have been in crisis mode responding to the immediate health and economic impacts of the pandemic, while at the same time seeing many of their processes and operations disrupted by the need to work, collaborate, and deliver services in a fully digital way like never before.
Once the immediate crisis has passed, the question remains as to what long-term changes to government and society will we see as a result? Will the "new normal" of the post-pandemic world see digital government efforts be accelerated with employees continuing to work remotely and services increasingly delivered online? This Policy Crunch will explore these issues and the impact that the pandemic response is having on related topics such as privacy and cyber security, the use of AI and data analytics, and the skills and competencies needed for government and citizens to effectively engage in a digital world.
This will be IOG's first fully online Policy Crunch as we follow current social distancing guidelines.
